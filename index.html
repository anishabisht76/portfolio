01About

Design first. Ship it clean.

I'm Anisha, a 3rd-year BCA student in Mumbai who got hooked on the moment a design turns into something you can actually click. I spend most of my time moving between two places: sketching a flow in Figma, and turning it into fast, accessible, pixel-careful front-end code.

I lead a dance team on weekends — planning formations, timing, and transitions for a room full of people. It turns out that's not so different from planning a layout: everything is about rhythm, spacing, and knowing exactly when something should move.

Right now I'm deepening my front-end craft with Node.js and Express on the backend side, leaning hard into UI/UX practice, and — yes — I vibe-code a good chunk of my prototypes before refining them by hand.

04How I work

Three moves, every project.

Same sequence whether it's a hackathon idea at midnight or a proper internship deliverable.

01

Listen & sketch

Understand who's using it and what they need to feel or do — then rough it out fast, on paper or in Figma.

02

Design the system

Palette, type scale, spacing, components — decided once, reused everywhere, so the UI stays consistent as it grows.

03

Build & polish

Clean semantic markup, real interactions, and a pass of "does this feel good to use" before it ever ships.
